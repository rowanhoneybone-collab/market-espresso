Interest rates, translated.

Think of rates as the price of money.

When rates are high, mortgages, car loans and business borrowing cost more. That can slow the economy. High bond yields also give investors a safer place to earn returns, so stocks—especially high-growth stocks—have to work harder to look attractive.

Rates ↑→Borrowing costs ↑→Growth can slow→Expensive stocks feel pressure
